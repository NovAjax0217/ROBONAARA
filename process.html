DESIGN & PLANNING

Ideas become sketches and plans. We work out the robot's structure, mechanisms, electronics and control systems.

03

BUILDING AJAX

The design becomes physical. We build the chassis, mechanisms and systems that make AJAX a real robot.

04

ELECTRONICS

Motors, controllers, sensors and wiring are connected so every part of AJAX can work together.

05
CODE

PROGRAMMING

Code transforms our strategy into robot behaviour, controls and precise movements on the field.

06
IMPROVE

TEST & DEBUG

We test AJAX, find problems, fix them and repeat until the robot becomes faster, stronger and more reliable.

07
DEPLOY

COMPETITION READY

After designing, building, coding and testing, AJAX is ready to leave the workshop and take on the competition field.

ROBONAARA ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

BUILD → TEST → FAIL → IMPROVE → REPEAT

Robotics isn't about getting everything right on the first attempt. It's about learning from every problem, improving the design and coming back stronger.
