HOW WE BUILT AJAX

FROM AN IDEA TO A COMPETITION ROBOT

01 — IDEA

THE CONCEPT

Everything begins with an idea. We study the challenge, discuss strategies and figure out what AJAX needs to do on the field.

02 — DESIGN

DESIGN & PLANNING

We turn our ideas into designs, deciding the structure, mechanisms, electronics and control systems before construction begins.

03 — BUILD

BUILDING AJAX

Components become a robot. We build the chassis, mechanisms and systems while constantly checking that everything fits together correctly.

04 — ELECTRONICS

POWER & CONTROL

Motors, controllers, sensors and wiring are connected and tested so every part of AJAX can communicate and work together.

05 — CODE

PROGRAMMING

We program AJAX to respond to our controls and execute the movements and strategies required during competition.

06 — TEST

TESTING & DEBUGGING

Things don't always work the first time. We test, find problems, fix them and test again until AJAX becomes more reliable.

07 — READY

COMPETITION READY

After countless adjustments, tests and improvements, AJAX is prepared to face the competition.

BUILD → TEST → FAIL → IMPROVE → REPEAT

That's how ROBONAARA builds.
