ROBONAARA

ROBONAARA is a student robotics team from Orchids The International School, Tathawade. We design, build and program robots while constantly experimenting, testing and improving.

OUR MISSION

Build a robot that doesn't just work in the lab, but performs when it matters. Every failure teaches us something new and every improvement brings AJAX closer to competition readiness.
